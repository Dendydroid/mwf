Quick reference for testing

What you can say during a call

Everything you can try in a test call, and what you should hear back. Speak English, German, Russian or Ukrainian. The assistant answers in the language of your last sentence. Anything in the first section works at any moment, even mid-booking.

You want toTry sayingYou should hear
At any time during the call
Hear the weather “What's the weather in Berlin?”
“Какая погода в Берлине?”		 The current weather in Berlin, in plain words.
Know the euro rate “How many hryvnias is one euro?”
“Wie steht der Euro zur Hrywnja?”		 Today's official euro to hryvnia rate.
Ask about a date “What date is next Saturday?” An apology: it can't help with dates. It never names one.
Hear the last answer again “Sorry, can you say that again?” The last answer, word for word.
Switch language “Und wie ist das Wetter in Berlin?” The answer in German. Every answer follows the language you just spoke.
Ask for something it can't do “Hello!” · “Weather in Paris?” · “Dollar rate?” It can't help with that, followed by what it can do: the weather, the euro rate and booking a doctor.
Answer “Anything else?” “Yes.” · “No, that's all.” Yes: it asks what you need. No: it says goodbye.
Talk to a person “Can I speak to an operator?” It says it is putting you through. The test page then hangs up.
End the call “That's all, thank you. Bye.” A goodbye, and the call ends. An unfinished booking is dropped.
Booking a doctor's appointment · four details, each read back to you
Start a booking “I'd like to see a doctor.” The first question: the patient's full name.
Answer the question “Anna Weber.” · “The 3rd of May 1990.” Your answer read back: “Is the name Anna Weber?” An impossible date is asked for again.
Say it's right “Yes, that's right.” The next question.
Say it's wrong “No, that's wrong.” The same question again. After three noes for one detail, it offers you a person.
Correct it right away “No, it's Anna Becker.” The new value, read back.
Fix an earlier answer “Actually, my birthday is the 4th of May.” Only that detail is changed and read back. Everything else stays, and the booking carries on.
Skip the “yes” Asked to confirm the date of birth: “It's for a headache.” The date is taken as right, and your reason is read back.
Point to what you said “Same name as I told you before.” The value from earlier in the call, read back.
Hear a detail again “Can you repeat my name?”
“What date of birth did you note?”		 What it has recorded so far, then the booking question you were on.
Check the booking “Is my appointment booked?” Not yet, until every detail is confirmed. With no booking open, it says there is none.
Ask something else “Wait, what's the weather in Berlin?” The answer, then the booking question again. Nothing is lost.
Cancel “Forget it, cancel the appointment.” The booking is dropped, and it asks if you need anything else.
Finish “Yes” to the last detail. The appointment is booked, then “Anything else?”

The booking asks, in this order

  1. Full name of the patient
  2. Date of birth · any spoken date, such as “3 May 1990”
  3. Reason for the visit
  4. Preferred appointment date · kept as you say it, such as “next Friday”

Testing tips

  • Open /mimic-client in Chrome and press Start call, or type and press Enter.
  • Each page load is a new call. Reload to start over.
  • Under each answer, the badges show what the assistant understood.
